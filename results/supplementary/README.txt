Supplementary research outputs for Paper 1.

These files contain pair-level model selection, candidate rankings, event-response profiles, transaction metrics, failure cases, and metadata needed to interpret the released results. They are generated outputs rather than the manuscript itself.

The primary temporal protocol is training 2010-2020, validation/model selection 2021-2023, OOS development 2024-2025, and final audit 2026. The final audit is not used for tuning.
